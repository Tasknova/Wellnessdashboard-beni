## 3. Regulatory Landscape

### Drug Licensing
- **Retail Drug License:** Required under Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940
- Registered pharmacist must be on premises during operating hours

### Drug Schedules

| Schedule | Control Level | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **H** | Prescription-only | Rx symbol on label; cannot sell without valid Rx |
| **H1** | Stricter subset | Separate register: prescriber + patient details; 3-year records |
| **X** | Maximum control | Special license, triplicate Rx, police verification |

*In practice, enforcement of Schedule H is weak — many chemists sell OTC.*

### E-Pharmacy Draft Rules
- Proposed **August 2018** — **still NOT enacted (7+ years)**
- Delhi HC (Nov 2023) directed government to frame policy — still pending
- Drugs, Medical Devices & Cosmetics Bill 2022 proposes mandatory e-pharmacy licensing
- **Biggest uncertainty for the industry**

### DPCO & NLEM (Price Controls)
- NPPA regulates ~900 NLEM formulations
- DPCO 2025: 743 scheduled formulations; WPI adjustment 0.64% for 2025
- Controlled drugs: fixed trade margin of **16%**
- Non-compliance triggers recovery of overcharged amount + interest

### GST on Medicines (Post Sep 2025)
- **0%:** 37 life-saving drugs (cancer, HIV, TB, CKD)
- **5%:** Most common medicines (antibiotics, painkillers, insulin, vaccines, ayurvedic, nutraceuticals)
- **12% slab:** Removed for most medicines
- **18%:** APIs, Key Starting Materials, chemical intermediates

## 4. Consolidation Trends

### M&A Activity
- Q1 2025: 71 deals worth INR 22,279 Cr in Pharma & Healthcare
- Q3 2025: $3.5 Bn in deals

### Why Standalone Pharmacies Join Chains
- Better procurement margins (bulk buying)
- Technology costs too high individually
- Brand recognition and trust
- Compliance burden increasing
- Access to digital/omnichannel capabilities

### Key Strategic Deals
- Apollo HealthCo + Keimed (INR 22,481 Cr EV) — largest integrated pharmacy-distribution entity
- Reliance Retail + Netmeds integration
- Tata 1mg: 180 → 3,000 stores planned

## 5. India-Specific Challenges

### Drug Counterfeiting
- **ASPA-CRISIL 2025:** Flags 28% counterfeit medicines in market
- Estimates range 5–25%; in major cities, **1 in 5** may be fraudulent
- DCGI mandated QR codes/barcodes on top 300 brands (Aug 2023)

### Price Controls & Thin Margins
- NLEM drugs under price control
- Margins 15–20% on branded drugs; thinner on controlled drugs
- Jan Aushadhi generic pressure (50–90% cheaper)

### Pharmacist Shortage
- Registered pharmacist required by law
- Growing demand vs declining enrollments
- Rural areas especially underserved

## 6. Government Initiatives

### Jan Aushadhi Kendras
- **17,990 operational** (Dec 2025); target **25,000 by March 2027**
- From 80 stores (2014) to 17,990 — **210-fold expansion**
- Consumer savings exceeded INR 8,000 Cr in FY24-25
- Sells at **50–90% discount** vs branded equivalents

### Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)
- World's largest government health insurance
- Covers 10.74 Cr families (50 Cr beneficiaries)
- INR 5 L/family/year for hospitalization
- Increases demand for post-hospitalization medication

### ABDM & ABHA ID
- **73.98 Cr ABHA IDs** created (Feb 2025)
- 3.63 L health facilities registered
- 49 Cr health records linked
- Foundation for unified pharmacy platforms and e-pharmacy regulation

## 7. Future Outlook (2027–2030)

### Projections
- Pharmacy retail: **USD 48–51 Bn by 2030**
- Online pharmacy: **USD 14 Bn by 2034**
- Organized share: **25–30% by 2030** (from 8.5% today)

### Key Predictions
1. **Consolidation accelerates:** Top 5 chains will control 20–25% by 2030
2. **Omnichannel becomes mandatory:** Pure online or pure offline will lose
3. **E-pharmacy regulation arrives:** Clarity expected to unlock massive growth
4. **Jan Aushadhi disruption continues:** 25K+ government stores at 50–90% discounts
5. **Tier II/III growth:** 40%+ of new pharmacy growth from non-metro
6. **Integrated ecosystems:** Pharmacy + diagnostics + teleconsultation + insurance bundling (Apollo model) becomes norm
7. **Generic penetration increases:** Both government push and private chains driving adoption
