## 2. Margin Structure

### Retailer Margins by Category

| Category | Retailer Margin on MRP |
|---|---|
| Branded Rx medicines | 16–22% |
| Generic medicines | 20–50% |
| Trade generics (unbranded) | Up to 200–1000% markup |
| OTC products | 10–20% |
| FMCG / Personal care | 5–10% |
| Private label products | ~2x branded margins |

### Supply Chain Margin Stack (Branded)

```
Manufacturer → C&F Agent (4–8%) → Stockist (6–10%) → Distributor (8–12%) → Retailer (16–22% on MRP)
```

## 4. Generic vs Branded Economics

### Price Difference
- Jan Aushadhi generics are **50–90% cheaper** than branded equivalents
- Branded drugs carry 80–90% markups over production cost

### Market Composition (2023–24)
- Branded generics: ~85% of Indian Pharma Market by value
- Trade generics: ~10%
- Generic generics (unbranded/Jan Aushadhi): ~5%

### Retailer Margin Comparison

| Type | Retailer Margin |
|---|---|
| Branded | 16–22% |
| Generic | 20–50% |
| Trade generics | 200–1000%+ markup |

### Jan Aushadhi Scheme
- **15,479** operational Kendras (May 2025)
- Sales grew from INR 7.29 Cr (2014) to INR 1,470 Cr (2024) — 20,063% increase
- Many tier-3/4 Kendras operate at a loss despite INR 5 L government subsidies

### Private Label Strategy
- **MedPlus** private label: 22.2% of revenue, 1,100+ SKUs
- Pharma GMV share jumped to 18.9% from 7.9% YoY
- Targets 50–60% revenue from store generics within 3 years
- Private labels deliver **double the margin** vs branded

## 6. Pricing Dynamics

### MRP Regulation (DPCO 2013)
- NPPA fixes ceiling prices for drugs on NLEM (764 formulations as of Mar 2025)
- Controlled drugs: fixed trade margin of **16%**
- Non-scheduled drugs: annual MRP increase up to 10% permitted
- WPI adjustment for 2024–25: 1.74%

### PTR/PTS Calculation (post-GST)
```
PTR = (MRP - (MRP × Retail Margin%)) / (1 + GST%)
Standard retailer margin: ~20% on MRP
Standard stockist margin: 8–10% on PTR
```

### Discount Wars
- E-pharmacies offer **15–25% discounts** on MRP
- E-pharmacy penetration: ~11% in 2024 (up from 2–3% in 2019)
- Brick-and-mortar still dominates with 75% share
- AIOCD actively opposes e-pharmacy discounting

## Key Takeaways

1. **Revenue per store varies 3x** between scaled chains (INR 10–13 L/month) and premium-format (INR 30 L/month). Hospital-adjacent stores are the outlier at INR 2–3 L/day.

2. **Generic = margin unlock.** 20–50% retailer margins vs 16–22% for branded. Private label is the nuclear option — 2x branded margins. MedPlus proving this at scale (22% of revenue).

3. **Rx-to-OTC shift accelerating.** OTC share grew from 34.6% to 38.7% in one year. Regulatory reclassification + self-medication trends driving this.

4. **Organized pharmacy at inflection.** ~10% market share today, projected 25–30% by 2030. Massive consolidation opportunity.

5. **E-pharmacy disruptive but small.** ~11% penetration. Real battle: organized chains going omnichannel vs pure-play e-pharmacies.

6. **New stores take 12–18 months to break even.** Mature store EBITDA 10–12% vs 2–3% for new stores.

7. **Revenue per sqft is excellent** (INR 20K–60K) — better than most retail formats.
